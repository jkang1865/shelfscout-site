# ShelfScout Support

Welcome to ShelfScout support. We're here to help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add products to a store?**
Tap the camera button or "Add shelf photo" on any store page. 
Point your camera at a store shelf and ShelfScout's AI will 
automatically identify and add all visible products.

**Why don't I see my local store on the map?**
If your store isn't showing, you can add it manually. On the 
Map tab, tap "Add a store" and follow the steps to pin its location.

**A product was incorrectly identified. How do I fix it?**
Go to the store's shelf viewer, find the product in the list, 
and tap it. You'll see options to correct the product name or 
adjust its location on the shelf photo.

**How do I delete a product that shouldn't be there?**
In the shelf viewer, swipe left on any product to remove it.

**How do I delete my account?**
To request account deletion and removal of your data, please 
email us at the address below. We will process your request 
within 30 days.

**The app isn't finding stores near me.**
Try tapping "Search this area" on the Map tab after navigating 
to your neighborhood. This triggers a fresh search for stores 
in the visible map area.

**My shelf photo failed to upload.**
Make sure you have a stable internet connection and try again. 
Shelf photo analysis requires an active connection.
