# ShelfScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

ShelfScout ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. 
This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your 
rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you create an account, we collect your email address. If you sign in 
with Apple or Google, we receive basic profile information (name and email) 
from those providers.

**Location Data**
We collect your approximate location when you use the app to show nearby 
stores and products. Location is only accessed while the app is in use and 
is never shared with third parties.

**Photos**
When you take a shelf photo, it is uploaded to our secure servers and 
analyzed by AI to identify products. Photos are stored and displayed to 
other users as part of store inventory data.

**Usage Data**
We collect information about how you use the app, including products 
scanned, stores visited, and corrections made. This data improves the 
accuracy of our AI and the quality of store inventory information.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve the ShelfScout service
- To show you nearby stores and product inventory
- To process shelf photos and identify products using AI
- To maintain your account and contribution history
- To improve our AI models using anonymized, aggregated data

## AI and Third-Party Services

ShelfScout uses the following AI services to analyze shelf photos:

- **Google Gemini** — primary shelf photo analysis
- **OpenAI GPT-4o** — fallback shelf photo analysis

When you submit a shelf photo, the image is sent to these services for 
analysis. Please review Google's and OpenAI's privacy policies for 
information on how they handle image data.

We also use:
- **Supabase** — database and authentication
- **Google Places API** — store discovery
- **Foursquare** — store discovery

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with 
advertisers. We share data only with the third-party services listed 
above, which are necessary to operate the app.

Store inventory data (product names, store locations, shelf photos) 
is crowdsourced and visible to all ShelfScout users.

## Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. 
You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any 
time by contacting us at the email below.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Opt out of future communications

## Children's Privacy

ShelfScout is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly 
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of 
significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
