ShelfScout — Account & data deletion

This page explains how to delete your ShelfScout account and associated personal data. ShelfScout is available on the App Store and Google Play.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account in two ways:

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app Instant

  1. Open ShelfScout and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted — your account and associated personal data will be deleted immediately.

This is the fastest way to delete your account. No email required.

Option 2 — Email request

  1. Send an email from the address tied to your ShelfScout account (if possible) to jkang9903@gmail.com with the subject line: ShelfScout account deletion request.
  2. In the message, include the email address you used to sign in (or state that you signed in with Apple or Google and which provider you used).
  3. We will confirm receipt when reasonable and process your request in line with our privacy policy.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we remove or anonymize personal data tied to your account, including:

  • Your account profile (email and profile fields we store)
  • Your personal activity history associated with your account (e.g. scans and contributions stored as yours)
  • Any shelf photos you uploaded

What we may keep

ShelfScout is crowdsourced: some information you contributed (for example product names, store locations, or shelf photos that were shared as part of inventory) may be visible to other users in the app. Depending on how that data was stored, we may not be able to remove every past contribution from historical records without affecting other users' experience. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal identifiers where we can; for content that cannot be removed without breaking shared inventory, we may retain anonymized or aggregated information where permitted by law.

Retention

In-app deletion is processed immediately. Email requests are processed as soon as reasonably practicable. Residual copies in backups may take additional time to expire. If we must retain certain information for legal or security reasons, we will keep it only as long as required.
